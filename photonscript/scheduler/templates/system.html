How to capture each type
DARKS & BIAS — the button above. Prereq: roof CLOSED, night time (no shutter on the RC16 — any light ruins them). Cloudy/unsafe nights are ideal. ~3.5 h for 300s×20 + 600s×20 + 50 bias at the configured imaging gain / offset / 0°C. Refresh darks ~every 90 days.

FLATS (sky flats) — NINA Flat Wizard, run at dusk while the sun is −3° to −8° (roughly the 20 min after sunset): Wizard mode Sky flats → Multi Mode with all filters ON → Slew to zenith (East) → histogram mean 50%, tolerance 10% → 10–15 flats per filter. Re-shoot after any dust event, camera rotation, or focuser/optics change — and at least every 45 days. PhotonScript sees them automatically on the next health refresh.

Automating flats later: the Flat Wizard itself can't be scripted, but the Advanced Sequencer has a Flats instruction category (Sky Flat / Auto Exposure Flat / Trained Flat Exposure). Build a dummy advanced sequence containing those three, save it as JSON, and share the file — then dusk sky flats can be added to the scheduler automatically.

PHD2 Guide Guard (PS-91)

Watches PHD2 live for a lock on a hot pixel or other non-star, PHD2 guiding a parked or closed-roof scope, and max-length pulses that move nothing. Subs guided on a non-star are marked guide_lock WARN. The hot-pixel map is rebuilt automatically while the roof is closed before dusk; build it by hand only with the roof closed or the guide camera capped.

PHD2 Pulse Self-Test (PS-92)

Does the mount move on a guide pulse? PHD2 loops while PhotonScript pulses W, E, N, S (3 each) and measures how far the star field moved against the guide speed. PASS: every direction 0.5 to 1.5x of expected and opposite pairs oppose; WARN above 1.5x (guide-rate mismatch); FAIL under 0.5x or not opposing. With PS_PHD2_SELFTEST_ENABLED on, NINA runs it after the twilight autofocus and before each guided target. Run it by hand only with the scope tracking on a star field and PHD2 not guiding.

PHD2 Calibration (PS-93)

The calibration PHD2 uses now, graded. PASS: axes within 5 deg of perpendicular, RA/Dec rate ratio within 30% of cos(Dec), 8 or more steps per axis, taken near Dec 0 and the meridian. FAIL: failed, no motion, ortho over 5 deg, ratio off, parity changed. With PS_PHD2_CAL_MODE=auto a guided night calibrates (NINA slot PHD2_CALIBRATION after the twilight autofocus) only when this is missing, FAILED, older than PS_PHD2_CAL_MAX_AGE_DAYS, the PHD2 profile or binning changed, or you ask for it below.
