Plan a mosaic goal

A mosaic is one goal made of RC16 panel goals. Each panel has its own coordinates, filter plan and progress; the planner finishes the panels in order (P1 first) and hands the mount to the next panel when tonight's share of the current one is shot. Neither rig has a rotator, so the panels are laid out at the camera's own fixed angle ("camera" = measured from the RC16 plate solves). A galaxy-aligned grid needs the camera turned by hand first. The Piggy-600 rides along on its own goal (the companion): its subs during a panel credit that goal.

Grid (rows × cols): 1×2 2×1 2×2 2×3 3×2 3×3
