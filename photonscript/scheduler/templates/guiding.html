PHD2 Settings Audit (PS-89)

PHD2, its stored profile, the newest guide log, NINA's guider settings, the mount driver, TheSky and the PHD2 dark library against config/phd2/desired_oag_rc16.toml. Runs at every guided arm (one push only on a FAIL) and 60 s after a PHD2 configuration change. Apply does a dry run first and asks before changing anything: API rows (exposure, RA min-move, Dec guide mode) only while PHD2 is Stopped or Looping; profile rows need PS_PHD2_AUDIT_AUTOFIX, PHD2 closed and the armer idle; mount driver, TheSky and NINA rows are report only. PHD2 Pulse Self-Test (PS-92)

Does the mount move on a guide pulse? PHD2 loops while PhotonScript pulses W, E, N, S (3 each) and measures how far the star field moved against the guide speed. PASS: every direction 0.5 to 1.5x of expected and opposite pairs oppose; WARN above 1.5x (guide-rate mismatch); FAIL under 0.5x or not opposing. With PS_PHD2_SELFTEST_ENABLED on, NINA runs it after the twilight autofocus and before each guided target. Run it by hand only with the scope tracking on a star field and PHD2 not guiding.

PHD2 Calibration (PS-93)

The calibration PHD2 uses now, graded. PASS: axes within 5 deg of perpendicular, RA/Dec rate ratio within 30% of cos(Dec), 8 or more steps per axis, taken near Dec 0 and the meridian. FAIL: failed, no motion, ortho over 5 deg, ratio off, parity changed. With PS_PHD2_CAL_MODE=auto a guided night calibrates (NINA slot PHD2_CALIBRATION after the twilight autofocus) only when this is missing, FAILED, older than PS_PHD2_CAL_MAX_AGE_DAYS, the PHD2 profile or binning changed, or you ask for it below.

PHD2 Guide Guard (PS-91)

Watches PHD2 live for a lock on a hot pixel or other non-star, PHD2 guiding a parked or closed-roof scope, and max-length pulses that move nothing. Subs guided on a non-star are marked guide_lock WARN. The hot-pixel map is rebuilt automatically while the roof is closed before dusk; build it by hand only with the roof closed or the guide camera capped. Capture is refused while a night runs, while PHD2 guides, or while another PHD2 job holds it.

Guide Star Tuner (PS-90)

The RC16 agent measures the guide star after each settle and on a filter change (get_star_image plus PHD2's SNR, HFD and ErrorCode). Target: peak 60 to 80% of full scale, never clipped, SNR over 20, HFD 2 to 5 px, exposure 1 to 4 s. Mode observe records only; exposure steps PHD2's exposure (only to exposures the dark library has). Gain and binning are recommended for the next night and written pre-dusk only with PS_PHD2_AUDIT_AUTOFIX.

Tonight's Guide Log (PS-88)

The night's PHD2 guide log analysed: RMS (all and settled), dropped and saturated frames, dithers, and the top findings with a fix each. Read on load and on Refresh only (it parses the whole log).

TheSky / TPoint Audit (PS-104)

Report only: TheSky's settings (read through its TCP scripting with read-only scripts), the ASTAP Image Link check on the newest RC16 L frame, NINA's first-slew error from its Center log, and the TPoint record you enter below, against config/thesky/desired_rc16.toml. Nothing here writes TheSky, moves the mount or takes an image. Runs at arm and on Refresh. TPoint numbers, ProTrack, catalogs and the run binning are not in TheSky's scripting API: enter them after each TPoint session. Unknown = no source could read it.
